WHEN TO HIRE ME

  • You want a memorable, handcrafted website that stands out from generic SaaS templates.
  • You care about loading speed, performance scores, and clean readable source code.
  • You want to talk directly to the developer building your site, not three account managers.
  • You need custom e-commerce, interactive 3D, or an editorial layout that tells a story.

WHEN TO CHOOSE ANOTHER OPTION

  • You need a generic $50 WordPress template installed in one hour without custom code.
  • You want a large 50-person agency with round-the-clock shift staffing.
  • You need native iOS/Android mobile apps (Swift/Kotlin) rather than responsive web applications.

How do we start working together?

Click "HIRE ME" or "START A MISSION" to send your requirements via Gmail. Tell me about your project, target launch date, and references. I'll reply within 24 hours with a scope outline and timeline.

Do you take projects outside India?

Yes, over 60% of my clients are in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Europe. Communication happens asynchronously over email and WhatsApp, with scheduled video calls when needed.

Can my team edit content without code knowledge?

Yes. I organize your content into straightforward data configs or connect a lightweight headless CMS so you can easily add products, change text, or update photos anytime.

How are payments and milestones structured?

Standard terms are 50% upfront deposit to reserve your sprint date on my schedule, and the remaining 50% upon final delivery, QA sign-off, and domain launch.
